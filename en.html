ABOUT & CREDENTIALS

Understand the Mechanics, Ride With Less Effort

Moon · Kevin CASI Level 2 Snowboard & CSIA Level 1 Ski Instructor Former Instructor in Niseko (Japan), Whakapapa (NZ) & Vancouver

I’m Moon (Kevin), a dual-certified snowboard and ski instructor based at Mt Seymour in Vancouver. With over 300 days on snow and 150+ days of on-mountain teaching experience across Canada, New Zealand (Whakapapa), and Japan (Niseko), I coach riders ranging from young kids to adults.

Most riders hit a plateau because they copy body positions without understanding how center of mass interacts with the board or ski edge. In my lessons, I break down movement mechanics into clear, actionable steps—paired with instant video playback so you can see and feel every adjustment run after run.

💡 Instruction Languages: Fluent in English, Mandarin, and Cantonese for seamless communication on the hill.
